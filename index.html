A FEW MINUTES. A FRESH START.

Your first cup, made simple.

Matcha is finely ground green tea,
whisked into water instead of steeped.

01 / THE CLASSIC

Traditional matcha

A small bowl. A whisk. A moment to yourself.

  1. Sift about 2 g of matcha into a bowl.
  2. Pour in 60–80 ml of water around 75–80°C.
  3. Whisk in a quick W motion until lightly frothy.
  4. Sip slowly. Adjust water to your taste.
02 / OVER ICE

Iced matcha latte

Cool, creamy, and entirely your own.

  1. Sift & whisk 2 g of matcha with 40 ml warm water.
  2. Fill a glass with ice and 150–200 ml milk of choice.
  3. Pour the matcha over the milk.
  4. Stir and add your preferred sweetener, if you like.

A little tip: sift first for a smoother cup, and use warm rather than boiling water.
