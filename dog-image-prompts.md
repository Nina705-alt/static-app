## dog-hero.png

A cheerful golden retriever with natural realistic anatomy, sitting in a warm cream kitchen, looking toward camera, next to a matte terracotta orange dog bowl with kibble and a plain unbranded sage green kraft dog food bag. Soft morning sunlight and organic shadows, cream plaster wall, warm oak floor, editorial lifestyle photography with tactile materials. Frame dog fully including ears, dog slightly right of center, plenty of breathing room. Warm inviting mood. No text, logos, badges, endorsements, people or medical claims.
